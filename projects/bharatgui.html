BharatGUI

LinesRL EnvironmentsEvaluation

GUI agents use a computer the way a person does: they read a screenshot, then click and type. Much Indian software, like state portals, has no API, so an agent that helps someone who does not read English has to work on the screen. Our Indian analogue of InfiniteWeb builds Indian websites over the same backends as our tool-calling environments. We have built two full replicas so far: a GST-style portal with 191 pages in English and Hindi, and a railway-booking site. Together they hold 226 tasks, and our reference solution passes every one.

Part of BharatGym, with Tool-calling Environments and Karobaar Gym.
