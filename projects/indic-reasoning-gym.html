Indic Reasoning Gym

LinesRL EnvironmentsDatasetsModels

Our Indian analogue of Reasoning Gym. Generators produce any number of reasoning problems, set in Indian contexts and languages, each with an answer a program can check. A model can train on fresh problems it has never seen, and a benchmark built from it cannot leak into training data.
