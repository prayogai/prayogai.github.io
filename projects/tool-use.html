Tool-calling Environments

LinesRL EnvironmentsEvaluation

Our Indian analogue of Agent World Model. We generate the backend of an Indian app or website: its database, the tools (API calls) an agent can use, and the rules of its domain, such as when Tatkal booking opens or how refunds work. Tatkal is the last-minute railway quota that opens a day before travel. A tool-calling agent gets a task in plain language, in English, Hinglish or an Indian language. When it stops, code checks the database: the ticket is booked or it is not.

Part of BharatGym, with BharatGUI and Karobaar Gym.
