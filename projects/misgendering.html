Misgendering in English-to-Hindi Translation

Part ofAI SafetyEvaluation

Hindi marks gender on verbs and adjectives, so a system that translates from English must often choose a gender that the English sentence leaves open. We measure how often translation systems misgender people when they translate from English to Hindi, and we test ways to reduce it.
