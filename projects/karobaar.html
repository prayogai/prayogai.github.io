Karobaar Gym

LinesRL EnvironmentsModelsEvaluation

An AI agent runs a simulated Indian business for weeks. The agent is the seller. Customers, suppliers and prices come from a simulator that draws them from realistic distributions, and the books are kept honestly. In KiranaBench, four open models ran a kirana shop (a neighbourhood grocery) for 30 days. One made money, ₹13,683, while a scripted shopkeeper made ₹1,03,434 in the same shops. Because the books are honest, profit is a reward we can train on: a Qwen3.5-4B we trained now makes 83% of the scripted owner's weekly profit on shops it never saw.

Part of BharatGym, with Tool-calling Environments and BharatGUI.
