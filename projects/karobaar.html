Karobaar Gym

Part ofRL EnvironmentsModelsEvaluation

An AI agent runs a simulated Indian business for weeks, such as a kirana shop (a neighbourhood grocery) or a truck fleet. The agent is the seller. Customers, suppliers and prices come from a simulator that keeps honest books, so the business's profit is both a score for testing and a reward for training.

Part of BharatGym, with Seva Gym and BharatGUI.
