Evaluation & Benchmarking

We test models on Indian tasks in Indian languages: frontier models through their APIs, and open models on our own servers. Most of our environments are also benchmarks. A task keeps the same records and the same checker in every language, so a score in Tamil can be compared fairly with a score in English.
