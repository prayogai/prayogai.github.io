Models

We fine-tune open models such as Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama and Gemma on our datasets and RL environments: at 4B and 8B parameters, and up to about 70B. The aim is small, cheap models that do Indian tasks well, in Indian languages.

How a model learns in a business environment

The model runs a practice shop. At each step, a scripted shopkeeper marks the model's decision and says what it would have done instead. The model trains on these corrections, and then runs new shops on its own.
