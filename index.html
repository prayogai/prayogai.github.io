हिन्दी · Hindi

Prayog AI is the Bharat AI Prayogshala at BITS Pilani. Prayogshala is Hindi for laboratory, and prayog means experiment. We work on AI for India across seven pillars: Indic datasets and RL environments, open models trained on them, benchmarks for frontier and open models, and research on safety, interpretability and self-improvement.

Seven pillars

We work across seven pillars

Our datasets and environments train models. Our benchmarks test them. Safety and interpretability research looks at how they behave and what happens inside them, and self-improvement feeds what we learn back in. Many projects serve several pillars at once.

What makes it Indian

Translation changes the language, not the context

A Western train-booking task translated into Hindi still has fares in dollars and Western refund rules. Our tasks depend on Indian context as well as Indian languages.

How people writeScripts, Hinglish, honorifics: aap, ji, bhaiya.
What everyone knowsA PNR, an IFSC code, an address that says “behind the Hanuman temple”.
What people actually doApprove a UPI collect request, race for a Tatkal ticket, raise a GST invoice for each sale.
What people valueA Jain meal with no onion or garlic, a lower berth for an elderly parent, a purchase timed to a muhurat.
