What makes it Indian

The same task, two ways

A Western task, translated into Hindi

“जॉन के लिए न्यूयॉर्क से बोस्टन का ट्रेन टिकट बुक कीजिए।”

Book a train ticket from New York to Boston for John.

  • The fare is in dollars
  • American names and cities
  • An American railway's refund rules
→

Our task

“मम्मी के लिए दिल्ली से जयपुर का Tatkal टिकट बुक कर दो, उन्हें lower berth चाहिए।”

Book a Tatkal ticket from Delhi to Jaipur for my mother. She needs a lower berth.

  • Tatkal: the last-minute quota that opens a day before travel
  • A lower berth for an elderly parent
  • A PNR to track the booking, and UPI to pay
